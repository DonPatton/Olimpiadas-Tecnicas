Frequently Asked Questions
How do I add a new product to inventory?
Use the "Add New" button on the Inventory view. Stock levels sync automatically to any connected marketplace channel.
Why is a marketplace listing showing a sync error?
Usually the channel's API key expired or a required field is missing. Check the channel card on the Marketplace view for details.
How are low stock thresholds calculated?
Each SKU has a reorder point you can set per warehouse. When quantity drops below it, the item is flagged as Low Stock.
Can I export orders and reports?
Yes — both the Orders and Reports views include an export option for the selected date range.
